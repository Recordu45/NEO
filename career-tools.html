Interview Preparation

Practice common interview questions before your next interview.

1. Tell me about yourself.

Keep your answer short and focus on education, relevant skills, experience and career goal.

2. Why should we hire you?

Explain the skills and experience you can bring to the role.

3. What are your strengths?

Mention genuine strengths that are relevant to the job.

4. What is your career goal?

Explain a realistic professional direction and how you plan to grow.

5. Why do you want this job?

Connect your skills and interests with the requirements of the role.
